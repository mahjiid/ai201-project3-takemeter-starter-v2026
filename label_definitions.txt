# TakeMeter label definitions for r/NBATalk.
# baseline.py reads this file, so these definitions match the README taxonomy.

analysis: advances a basketball claim and supports it with at least one specific checkable fact, statistic, record, date, quote, or piece of basketball evidence used to justify the claim
hot_take: advances a confident evaluative basketball claim or ranking without specific checkable support for the central claim
reaction: primarily reports or responds to a recent NBA event, quote, result, image, or news item with short commentary or emotion instead of building a sustained argument
discussion_question: primarily invites readers to compare, rank, predict, choose, or explain something without the author committing to a substantive answer of their own
